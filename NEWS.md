# xtpqroot 1.0.0

* Initial CRAN release.
* Implements the CIPS(tau) quantile panel unit root test following Yang, Wei &
  Cai (2022) and Nazlioglu et al. (2026).
* Implements the tFR panel unit root test (fractional Fourier + LST) following
  Corakci & Omay (2023).
* Monte Carlo p-values for CIPS(tau); sieve bootstrap for tFR.
